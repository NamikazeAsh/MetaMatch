# Competitive Concepts

## How to Beat Stall Teams
Stall teams rely on passive damage (Toxic, Hazards), recovery moves, and high defense to wear you down. To beat them, you need **Stallbreakers**.
1. **Knock Off + Hazards**: Removing Heavy-Duty Boots or Leftovers with Knock Off makes stall Pokemon vulnerable to Spikes and Stealth Rock.
2. **Taunt & Encore**: Fast Taunt users (like Heatran or Ogerpon) prevent walls from using Recover or Toxic. Encore locks them into passive moves.
3. **Setup Sweepers**: Pokemon with 'Substitute' or 'Stored Power' can set up on passive walls. Examples: Calm Mind Latios, Swords Dance Gliscor.
4. **Wallbreakers**: Use massive damage dealers like Choice Specs Enamorus or Choice Band Hoopa-Unbound to 2HKO walls.
5. **Mixed Attackers**: Pokemon that use both Physical and Special moves (like Iron Valiant) can break specific defensive cores (e.g., Blissey + Dondozo).

## Hyper Offense (HO) Guide
Hyper Offense focuses on overwhelming the opponent quickly with high-damage threats, often sacrificing defense for momentum.
1. **Suicide Leads**: Start with a dedicated lead (e.g., Glimmora, Deoxys-Speed, Ribombee) to set Stealth Rock/Spikes or Sticky Web, then faint to bring in a sweeper safely.
2. **Screens**: Light Clay Alolan Ninetales or Grimmsnarl set Reflect/Light Screen to help frail sweepers survive and set up.
3. **Setup Sweepers**: The core of HO. Pokemon like Kingambit, Dragonite, or Roaring Moon that use Dragon Dance/Swords Dance to win in one turn.
4. **Spinblocking**: Use a Ghost-type (Gholdengo) to prevent the opponent from removing your hazards.

## Teambuilding Checklist
1. **Win Condition**: How does this team win? (e.g., "Clear checks for Kingambit to sweep").
2. **Speed Control**: Do you have a Choice Scarf user or priority moves (Sucker Punch, Extreme Speed) for fast threats?
3. **Hazard Control**: Do you have a way to set Rocks? Do you have a way to remove them (Rapid Spin, Defog) or are you using Heavy-Duty Boots?
4. **Defensive Core**: (For Balance/Bulky Offense) Do you have resistances to common types like Ground, Fairy, and Fire?
5. **Role Compression**: Try to fit multiple roles on one mon. Example: Great Tusk (Spinner + Ground Check + Physical Wall).

## Common Archetypes
1. **Balance**: A mix of defensive pivots and offensive threats. Relies on safe switching.
2. **Bulky Offense**: Tanky attackers (Kingambit, Raging Bolt) that trade hits.
3. **Weather**: Sun (Torkoal + Protosynthesis), Rain (Pelipper + Swift Swim), Snow (Aurora Veil).
