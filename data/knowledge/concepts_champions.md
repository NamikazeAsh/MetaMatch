# Pokemon Champions Concepts

Champions singles (Champions OU and UU).

## How Champions Differs from Scarlet and Violet
Champions battles are at level 50, and spreads use Stat Points instead of EVs (up to 32 points in one stat). There is no Terastallization: Mega Evolution is the battle gimmick, and only one Pokemon per team can Mega Evolve in a battle. The available Pokemon, moves and items differ from SV, so SV analyses and SV usage numbers do not apply. Check the Champions usage stats for what is actually common.

## Mega Evolution Planning
A Mega Stone takes the item slot, so the Mega gives up Leftovers, Choice items or Heavy-Duty Boots. The Pokemon Mega Evolves before moving on the turn it is chosen, and its new ability and Speed apply that turn. Because only one Mega can be used per battle, teams usually build around one Mega and keep the rest of the team supporting its plan. Revealing which Pokemon holds the Mega Stone tells the opponent a lot, so leading with it is a commitment.

## Building a Champions Team
Start from the Mega you want to use and ask what stops it: which common Pokemon resist its attacks or outspeed it. Add teammates that remove those checks, set or remove hazards, and cover the Mega's weaknesses. Use the usage cards to see which items, spreads and moves are standard for each Pokemon, and the community guides for how players use specific Megas.
